THE MEMERADAR STANDARD

A green check is not a guarantee.

Automated scanners can miss malicious behavior, and results can be stale or incomplete. We show the provider's evidence without turning it into a promise of safety.

TOKEN SECURITY SCAN

Inspect a contract.

GoPlus Token Security is queried when the chain is supported and the provider responds.

WHAT WE CHECK

Evidence, not badges.

01Market signals

Liquidity, volume, buys/sells and price changes are sourced from market data. These do not prove safety.

02Contract flags

GoPlus may report honeypot flags, taxes, mint capability, ownership patterns and other checks where supported.

03Unknown means unknown

If a provider omits a field, we show “Unknown”. We never convert missing data into a clean result.

04Verify independently

Open the explorer, verify the exact contract and check official project links before making decisions.

Provider limitations

Coverage and availability vary by chain and contract. GoPlus responses are third-party automated screening, not an independent audit. This app does not execute buy/sell simulations or inspect every holder wallet. A failed request is not a clean scan.

LEARN TO READ THE MARKET

Radar academy.

Short, practical lessons to turn noisy token feeds into a repeatable research process.

LEARNING MODE
YOUR LEARNING LEVEL01 · Observer0 local XP earned through lessons
01 / FOUNDATION
◫

Liquidity is your exit door.

Thin pools can make even small trades move price sharply. Check pool depth and understand that displayed liquidity is not guaranteed to remain.

02 / MARKET STRUCTURE
↗

Volume needs context.

High volume can mean attention, churn, wash activity or distribution. Compare volume with liquidity and transaction balance instead of reading it alone.

03 / SECURITY
⬡

Contract risk is different.

A popular token can still have dangerous permissions. Review mint, freeze, blacklist, tax and sellability evidence where a reliable provider supports it.

04 / PROCESS
▤

Write the invalidation.

Before acting on an idea, record why it matters, what could prove you wrong, and what you still don't know. Avoid decisions driven by a green number.

Not a signal service.

MemeRadar does not tell you what to buy or sell. The Academy teaches research habits, not financial outcomes. XP is a local engagement feature and has no monetary value.

ABOUT MEMERADAR

Research with your eyes open.

MemeRadar is a read-only discovery and research terminal. It uses public market data and optional third-party security results. Wallet connection is optional and read-only. Clicking Connect Wallet opens the approval request from a real installed wallet; MemeRadar never creates demo wallets, executes trades or stores private keys.
